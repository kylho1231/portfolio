MotoRelease — Yamaha OR/CR & License Plate Tracking System

A web-based document and plate release management portal engineered for motorcycle dealerships (pilot implementation for a Yamaha dealership in San Jose de Buenavista, Antique). Digitizes the independent lifecycles of Official Receipts (OR), Certificates of Registration (CR), and metal license plates with Philippine SMS alerts (Semaphore), QR code access, masked RA 10173 data privacy gates, and double-claim prevention locks.

  • MySQL

EcoCollect SJ — Municipal Solid Waste Collection & MENRO Portal

A civic technology platform modernizing solid waste collection, citizen reporting, route progress tracking, and administrative governance for the Municipality of San Jose de Buenavista, Antique (Pilot Barangay: Funda-Dalipe). Enforces an honest data philosophy where unconfirmed timetables remain transparently marked Awaiting Official Confirmation, provides full RA 9003 waste segregation education in local Kinaray-a (Madunot, Mabaligya, Di-madunot, Delikado), and protects resident privacy under RA 10173.

  • Kotlin (Compose)
  • Next.js 15
  • TypeScript
  • Tailwind CSS
  • Cloud Firestore
  • Material Design 3
  • RA 9003 Waste Guide
  • Clean Architecture

AntiqueSupply — B2B Marketplace & Supplier Operations Platform

A full-stack regional B2B procurement marketplace engineered to connect local agricultural cooperatives and artisanal producers across Antique Province with commercial buyers, resorts in Boracay Island, and Panay restaurants. Features atomic row-level inventory locking in PostgreSQL/Supabase, multi-item Request for Quotation (RFQ) pipelines, order fulfillment state machines, and a companion native Android application with offline Room draft caching.

04 / THE WAY I WORK

INTENTION BEFORE IMPLEMENTATION.

Think it through.
Build it well.
Make it better.

A practical process, with people at the center.
Open a step to see how I approach it.

✳
01

Understand the need

+

Start with the people using the product. Understand the everyday task, the friction, and what a useful result would look like.

02

Give the idea a shape

+

Map the important flows and information. Explore the interface before committing to code, with clear navigation and simple decisions.

03

Build with intention

+

Develop in small, manageable steps. Connect the interface and application logic, keeping clarity and maintainability in mind.

04

Review, refine, repeat

+

Walk through the experience, test the important interactions, and address what gets in the way. Small refinements make a difference.
